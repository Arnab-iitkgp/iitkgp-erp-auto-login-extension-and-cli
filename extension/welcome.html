IIT KGP ERP Auto-Login

You're all set.

The extension is installed. Open the popup from your toolbar to enter your credentials - after that, ERP login is fully automatic.

How it works

When you visit erp.iitkgp.ac.in, the extension fills your roll number, password, and security answer automatically. If you've linked your erp gmail or institute email (preferred for fast otp fetch), it reads the OTP from your inbox and completes the login without any manual input. No complex OAuth or IMAP setup needed, just ensure the email is logged in within the same browser.

You can pause auto-login or switch to manual OTP entry anytime from the popup toggle.

Keyboard shortcuts

Open ERP & auto-login Alt + X
CDC Notice Board Alt + C
CDC Applications Alt + Z

Privacy

Everything runs locally. Your credentials stay in your browser's storage and are only sent to erp.iitkgp.ac.in and mail.google.com. No external servers, no tracking.

Refer FAQs at the bottom of the extension popup for any Troubleshooting/issues.
