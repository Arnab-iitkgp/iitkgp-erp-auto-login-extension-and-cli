How Auto-Login Works

Fully Automated
  • Setup once in the extension popup: Enter your Roll Number, Password, 3 security questions, and linked Gmail.
  • Hands-free Sign In: Whenever you open ERP, it automatically fills your credentials, answers the security question, grabs the OTP from your signed-in Gmail, and signs you in.
  • Always in your control: You can pause or resume Auto-Login anytime using the toggle switch in the popup, or switch to Manual OTP entry.

100% Local & Private

The extension reads the OTP email locally from your active Gmail session. Your credentials and emails never leave your machine — zero external servers, zero telemetry, and zero tracking.
